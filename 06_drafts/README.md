## Drafting and revision workflow

1. Run a full continuity pass across the 24 chapters.
2. Reconcile causal mechanics, travel timing, and the Return Interlock.
3. Check character development and the gradual evolution of Adrian and Mira's relationship.
4. Tighten pacing and remove repeated explanations.
5. Revise scientific language so established science and speculative mechanisms remain distinct.
6. Edit each chapter for voice, dialogue, and scene-level tension.
7. Assemble the revised manuscript.

## Style target

Close third-person narration, primarily through Adrian and Mira. Scientific details should be concrete and understandable without turning scenes into lectures. Emotional moments should emerge through action, disagreement, small choices, and what the characters avoid saying.
