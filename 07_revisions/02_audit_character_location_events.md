# Audit 1 — Character, Location, and Event Continuity

**Scope:** all 24 prose drafts in `06_drafts/`  
**Status:** reviewed on `iteration/audit09102026`  
**Severity:** P0 = blocks continuity; P1 = important; P2 = polish

## High-priority findings and resolutions

### P1 — War duration was contradictory
- **Before:** Chapter 1 implied years had passed since the invasion, while Chapter 3 said the invasion began less than a year ago.
- **Resolution applied:** Chapter 3 now says the invasion began nearly twenty years ago. Chapter 1's “the next years” phrasing remains consistent with that duration.
- **Why it matters:** The ending explicitly says Adrian and Mira remember almost twenty years of war. This is now seeded earlier rather than appearing as a new fact in Chapter 24.

### P1 — “First Veyr arrival” conflicted with a Veyr vessel already near Earth
- **Before:** Chapters 17 and 19 placed Mira before the “first Veyr arrival,” but Chapter 20 has a Veyr vessel already operating in Earth's local system.
- **Resolution applied:** Chapters 17 and 19 now specify the “first recorded hostile arrival at Earth.” This allows an undetected survey vessel to be present without Earth knowing of extraterrestrial life or the invasion having begun.

### P1 — Mira's return appeared both successful and unconfirmed
- **Before:** Chapter 23 appeared to send Mira directly back to AION, while Chapter 24 said Adrian had no confirmed arrival and later found her independently.
- **Resolution applied:** Chapter 23 now distinguishes the **causal reference** from a **physical address**. The interlock identifies AION's reference but cannot guarantee the traveller's arrival coordinates. Chapter 24 explains that Mira returns to the baseline history at a separate stable calibration site and follows the residual phase anomaly to Adrian.
- **Continuity rule to retain:** The interlock preserves causal continuity; it does not guarantee a return to the same room.

## Remaining author decision

The 20-year war is now established, but the prose shows only selected episodes rather than the full duration. This is acceptable if the narrative implies a long history before Chapter 1; if not, add one or two unobtrusive references to earlier years of shared work and losses. Avoid inserting a large new time jump into the AION/virus sequence, where resource constraints currently imply weeks or months.
