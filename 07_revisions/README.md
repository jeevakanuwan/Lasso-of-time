# Revisions

## Revision 002 — Restored-timeline ending

### Changed

1. Mira becomes the active temporal traveler.
2. The virus is deployed before the Veyr invasion.
3. Deployment occurs above Earth.
4. Earth does not yet know extraterrestrial life exists.
5. Veyr FTL/warp transport becomes the transmission vector.
6. The Veyr civilization collapses.
7. Human civilization reappears.
8. Adrian discovers his restored-timeline identity exists.
9. Mira also exists independently.
10. They have never met in the restored timeline.
11. Adrian searches for Mira.
12. Their reunion reveals both retain memories of the erased war.
13. Nearly twenty years of wartime life has no public historical record.
14. They leave documented history voluntarily.
15. They travel to a non-documented human era and live together until death.

### Removed

The earlier ambiguous ending in which humanity's ultimate fate remained uncertain.

### New thematic focus

**The greatest sacrifice is to save a world that will never know you saved it.**
