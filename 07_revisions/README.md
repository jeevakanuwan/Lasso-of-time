# Revisions

## Revision 002 — Canonical ending

### Established

1. Mira becomes the active temporal traveller.
2. She deploys LANTERN before the first recorded hostile arrival at Earth.
3. Her deployment point is in the sky above Earth; Earth has no knowledge of extraterrestrial civilization at this point.
4. LANTERN enters the Veyr's biological logistics and spreads through infected personnel and biological cargo carried between worlds by FTL transport. FTL is the logistics pathway, not a magical infection beam.
5. The Veyr's imposed temporal operation collapses when its distributed anchors lose coherence.
6. Human baseline history reasserts itself.
7. The Veyr civilization survives in the restored baseline history, but LANTERN causes heavy casualties. The species is not universally erased.
8. Adrian exists in the restored timeline.
9. Mira also exists independently in that timeline; they have never met in restored history.
10. Adrian searches for Mira and they reunite by following a residual phase anomaly and checking evidence from the closed causal loop.
11. Both retain memories of almost twenty years of a war no one else remembers.
12. They voluntarily leave documented history and live together in an undocumented era of human prehistory until the end of their lives.
13. The final line remains exactly: **“The universe forgot their war. They did not.”**

### Equation payoff

The equation that appears before Adrian derives it is a deliberate closed causal loop. Adrian and Mira later use the Veyr device to send it back to the earlier point where Adrian finds it in his notebook. It has no identifiable first author. The loop pays off the title *The Lasso of Time*.

### Thematic focus

**The greatest sacrifice is to save a world that will never know you saved it.**
