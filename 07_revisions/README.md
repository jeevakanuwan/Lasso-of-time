# Revisions

## Revision 002 — Canonical ending

### Established

1. Mira becomes the active temporal traveler.
2. She deploys the virus before the Veyr invasion of Earth.
3. Her deployment point is in the sky above Earth.
4. Earth has no knowledge of extraterrestrial civilization at this point.
5. The Veyr's FTL / warp transport network carries infected personnel and biological cargo between worlds.
6. The Veyr civilization collapses.
7. Human history reasserts itself.
8. Adrian discovers that he exists in the restored timeline.
9. Mira also exists independently in that timeline.
10. They have never met in the restored timeline.
11. Adrian searches for Mira and they reunite.
12. Both retain memories of almost twenty years of a war no one else remembers.
13. They voluntarily leave documented history.
14. They live together in an undocumented era of human prehistory until the end of their lives.

### Removed

The earlier ambiguous ending in which humanity's ultimate fate remained uncertain.

### Thematic focus

**The greatest sacrifice is to save a world that will never know you saved it.**

## Open continuity questions

1. How exactly does the virus avoid detection before symptoms begin?
2. Why does the collapse of the Veyr temporal operation restore human history instead of creating another branch?
3. How can Adrian and Mira identify one another in the restored world without public records of their relationship?
4. How does the final journey into prehistory work without creating a new temporal catastrophe?
5. How much subjective time passes inside AION, and how does that add up to nearly twenty years of shared experience?
