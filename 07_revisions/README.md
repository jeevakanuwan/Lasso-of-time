# Revisions

## Revision 002 — Canonical ending

### Established

1. Mira becomes the active temporal traveler.
2. She deploys the virus before the first recorded hostile arrival at Earth.
3. Her deployment point is in the sky above Earth.
4. Earth has no knowledge of extraterrestrial civilization at this point.
5. The Veyr's FTL / warp transport network carries infected personnel and biological cargo between worlds.
6. The Veyr's imposed temporal operation collapses when its distributed anchors lose coherence.
7. Human history reasserts itself.
8. Adrian exists in the restored timeline.
9. Mira also exists independently in that timeline.
10. They have never met in the restored timeline.
11. Adrian searches for Mira and they reunite.
12. Both retain memories of almost twenty years of a war no one else remembers.
13. They voluntarily leave documented history.
14. They live together in an undocumented era of human prehistory until the end of their lives.

### Removed

The earlier ambiguous ending in which humanity's ultimate fate remained uncertain.

### Thematic focus

**The greatest sacrifice is to save a world that will never know you saved it.**

## Remaining author decisions

1. **Veyr's ultimate fate:** confirm that the imposed temporal operation collapses without implying universal extermination in the restored baseline history.
2. **Bootstrap equation:** decide whether the equation that appears before Adrian derives it is a deliberately closed causal loop, and provide a payoff or explicitly preserve the mystery.
3. **AION name:** the technical note now defines “Observer Nullification” as nullifying the external network's authority over the observer's reference state.
4. **Twenty-year war:** duration now agrees across the prose. The story presents selected episodes from a war already underway; add earlier-history references only if the reader needs more scale.
5. **Prehistory:** keep their isolation deliberate and ethically meaningful; an undocumented era is not necessarily uninhabited.
