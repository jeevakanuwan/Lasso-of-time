# Chapter 1 — The Sky Was Already Occupied

## Purpose

Open during the alien war. Show that humanity has adapted to invasion without becoming accustomed to it.

## Key beats

- Adrian works in a subterranean research facility during an orbital attack.
- Mira arrives with biological samples recovered from a Veyr battlefield.
- Their first meaningful scientific disagreement establishes their personalities.
- The chapter ends with both realizing the Veyr may be studying more than human biology.
- A laboratory clock event records an effect before its apparent cause, foreshadowing temporal warfare.

## Emotional turn

Professional curiosity becomes personal fascination.

## Science focus

Alien material response, precision timing, reference clocks and anomalous event ordering.
