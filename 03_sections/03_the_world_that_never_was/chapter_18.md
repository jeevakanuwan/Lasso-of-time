# Chapter 18 — Two Humans Against a Universe

## Key beats

- They prepare the virus and the Veyr temporal device.
- Mira identifies the pre-invasion time window.
- Adrian prepares AION to preserve the information needed to understand what happens after she leaves.
- They agree that the goal is not simply to survive but to prevent the war.
- Their final moments together are shaped by the possibility that one of them may return to a history where the other is a stranger.
