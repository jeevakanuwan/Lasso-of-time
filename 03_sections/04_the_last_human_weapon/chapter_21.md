# Chapter 21 — The Prisoner from Tomorrow

## Key beats

- The Veyr capture Mira.
- They cannot understand why a human exists before humanity's documented contact with them.
- They identify the temporal anomaly but not the biological threat.
- Mira is transported aboard a Veyr vessel.
- She realizes the vessel's FTL system is connected to a huge network of ships and colonies.
- She understands the virus has found the perfect transmission mechanism.

## Critical scientific mechanism

The virus is not spreading through Earth's atmosphere alone.

It is spreading through **Veyr logistics**.

Their FTL network repeatedly moves biological personnel, medical supplies, food organisms and other living material between worlds.

The same transportation system that made the Veyr empire possible becomes the pathogen's distribution network.
