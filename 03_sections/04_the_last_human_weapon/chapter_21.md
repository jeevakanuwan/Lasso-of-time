# Chapter 21 — The Prisoner from Tomorrow

The Veyr capture Mira because they cannot understand why a human exists before documented human-Veyr contact.

They transport her aboard a Veyr vessel and eventually toward their modern civilization.

Their mistake is biological.

The virus has already entered their transportation system.

Their warp/FTL network repeatedly moves biological personnel, medical supplies and living material between worlds.

The transportation system that made the Veyr empire possible becomes the pathogen's distribution network.
