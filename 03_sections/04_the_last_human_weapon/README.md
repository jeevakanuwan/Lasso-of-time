# Section IV — The Last Human Weapon

This section now contains the definitive ending of the novel.

### Sequence

19. **The Boundary** — Mira discovers the real temporal opportunity.
20. **First Contact** — Mira travels to a pre-invasion Earth and deploys the virus from above the planet.
21. **The Prisoner from Tomorrow** — The Veyr capture her and unknowingly transport the virus through their FTL network.
22. **The Gift** — The pathogen reaches the Veyr civilization.
23. **The Last Outbreak** — The Veyr collapse and human history reasserts itself.
24. **The Lasso of Time** — Adrian discovers the restored world, searches for Mira, reunites with her, and they disappear into undocumented human prehistory.
