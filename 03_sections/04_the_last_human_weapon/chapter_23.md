# Chapter 23 — The Last Outbreak

## Key beats

- The Veyr infection becomes interstellar.
- Ships arrive carrying infected personnel before symptoms are recognized.
- Colonies report contradictory medical failures.
- Veyr temporal systems begin failing.
- The civilization's ability to maintain its altered causal history collapses.
- The human timeline reasserts itself.

## The visual transition

Adrian watches through AION as an alien Earth dissolves.

Then:

Cities.

Human traffic.

Aircraft.

Radio signals.

Human voices.

The world returns.

## Important distinction

The universe does not rewind like a film.

A new consistent history exists.

The people outside simply remember a world in which the war never occurred.
