# Chapter 23 — The Last Outbreak

## Key beats

- The infection becomes interstellar because infected Veyr personnel and biological cargo are repeatedly transported between systems.
- The virus has a long, initially low-symptom phase. Routine screening does not identify it as dangerous before infected ships depart.
- Veyr colonies report apparently unrelated failures in a biological pathway essential to their species.
- The outbreak reaches the Veyr personnel responsible for maintaining distributed temporal anchor nodes.
- Anchor nodes fall out of synchronization. The Veyr's imposed anti-human history can no longer remain stable.
- The rewritten history collapses, and the last self-consistent baseline timeline returns.

## The visual transition

Adrian watches through AION as the alien Earth loses coherence.

Then the signs of human civilization return: city lights, aircraft, radio traffic, satellites and human voices.

## Important distinction

The universe does not rewind like a film. The active history transitions from a Veyr-imposed causal solution to the baseline history that existed before their intervention.

People outside AION remember a world in which the war never happened.

Adrian retains the erased history because AION protected his causal reference. Mira retains it because her temporal transit preserved her connection to the Veyr device's reference state.
