# Chapter 23 — The Last Outbreak

The Veyr infection becomes interstellar.

Ships carry infected personnel before symptoms are recognized.

Colonies report contradictory failures.

Veyr temporal systems begin failing.

The civilization's ability to sustain the altered causal history collapses.

Adrian watches from AION as the alien Earth dissolves.

Then:

- cities,
- human traffic,
- aircraft,
- radio signals,
- human voices.

The world returns.

The universe does not rewind like a film. A new consistent history exists in which the war never happened.
