# Timeline Model — Canonical Explanation

## The Veyr intervention

The Veyr do not casually rewrite history. They impose a specific causal solution using a distributed network of temporal anchor nodes. The network continually synchronizes the altered history across the Veyr civilization.

The rewritten history makes humanity's rise impossible. From the perspective of the altered present, the Veyr have always controlled Earth.

## Why the virus can undo it

The virus attacks a fictional biological pathway that the Veyr need to maintain the living systems responsible for their temporal anchors. It does not directly destroy spacetime. It causes the anchor network to fail biologically and operationally.

Because the anchors must remain synchronized, failures propagate through the network. The Veyr cannot repair the nodes quickly enough to keep the imposed history stable.

When the network loses coherence, the imposed history ceases to be maintained. The universe settles into the baseline history that does not depend on the Veyr intervention.

## Why this is not a simple rewind

A rewind would return every object and person to an earlier state. That is not what happens.

Instead, the active history becomes the baseline history. Ordinary people remember only that history. Adrian's memory survives because AION isolated his causal reference. Mira's memory survives because she traveled through the Veyr device with a preserved temporal reference.

The two of them are continuity exceptions, not evidence that everyone remembers two timelines.

## The FTL network

FTL is not a conduit through which the virus magically travels. Infected Veyr personnel and biological cargo physically travel on ships. The speed and frequency of these trips let the pathogen reach many worlds during its low-symptom phase.

The same logistics system that makes the Veyr empire possible becomes the outbreak's distribution network.

## Reunion logic

Adrian cannot simply search for a girlfriend who never existed in his restored history. He searches for a chronometric signature recorded by AION.

Mira independently notices a matching anomaly in her research. Each follows the same unexplained signal through scientific work. Their eventual meeting is therefore caused by the choices they make after restoration.

The final proof is a private memory that has no public record and could not be inferred from their ordinary lives.

## Prehistory logic

The Veyr device can navigate to a chosen temporal coordinate, while AION's stored causal reference prevents them from losing their own continuity during transit.

They choose a remote place and an undocumented era, avoid known communities, and make no effort to become founders of a civilization or figures in mythology. “Undocumented” does not mean consequence-free; they deliberately minimize contact so the restored historical record remains intact.
