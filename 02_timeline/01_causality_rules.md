# Causality Rules

These rules are canon. Chapters may reveal them gradually, but must not contradict them.

1. Time travel is not ordinary transportation.
2. The Veyr can move matter and information along controlled temporal trajectories.
3. The Veyr's attempt to erase humanity is maintained by a distributed temporal anchor network. Their rewritten history is an imposed solution, not a naturally stable history.
4. AION preserves a local causal reference state. It does not preserve the outside universe.
5. AION occupants continue to experience normal time and retain memories when external history changes.
6. The Veyr's temporal network requires synchronized nodes distributed through their civilization.
7. The virus does not spread by magic through a warp field. It travels in infected Veyr personnel and biological cargo aboard FTL ships.
8. The virus is designed to exploit a fictional Veyr-specific biological dependency. Its incubation period is long enough for interstellar distribution before symptoms trigger containment.
9. Mira learns to operate the Veyr temporal device and travels to a period before the invasion of Earth.
10. Her deployment point is in the sky above Earth. Humanity does not yet know the Veyr exist.
11. When infection disables the Veyr's distributed temporal anchors, the imposed anti-human history loses its stabilizing mechanism.
12. The timeline then returns to the last self-consistent baseline history not dependent on the Veyr intervention: the human history in which the invasion never occurred. The Veyr civilization also survives in that history, but LANTERN causes heavy casualties; the imposed branch's collapse does not universally erase the Veyr species.
13. This is not a universal rewind. It is a transition from an imposed, actively maintained causal solution to the baseline solution.
14. Adrian's continuity is protected by AION. Mira's continuity is protected by her temporal transit and the Veyr device's causal lock.
15. Their ordinary selves exist in the restored history, but neither ordinary self remembers the war.
16. The two travelers retain the memories and subjective age accumulated during the erased war.
17. Adrian and Mira have never met in the restored history. Their reunion must result from active choices and clues, not coincidence alone.
18. The Veyr collapse does not automatically restore every object or memory from the erased timeline.
19. Adrian and Mira's final journey into prehistory uses the Veyr device with AION's causal reference as a navigational safeguard.
20. They choose an isolated place and avoid contact with communities that could alter documented history.
21. The equation found in Adrian's notebook is a closed causal loop. After their reunion, Adrian and Mira use the Veyr device to send it back to the earlier moment when it first appeared. It has no independent first author, and the characters deliberately close the loop.
22. **AION preserves observers, not history.**
