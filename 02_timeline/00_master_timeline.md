# Master Timeline

## Timeline A — The original war history

### T-12 years — First signs

Astronomers detect unexplained objects and energy events.

### T-10 years — First contact

The Veyr arrive.

### T-10 to T-1 years — The war

Humanity fights the Veyr.

Adrian and Mira meet through the international war-science program.

They fall in love.

### T-1 year — The virus

Mira's team identifies a conserved Veyr biological vulnerability.

The weapon is developed but cannot be safely deployed.

### T-8 months — Veyr temporal research

Human intelligence discovers evidence that the Veyr are experimenting with temporal displacement.

### T-3 months — AION

Adrian builds the first AION chamber.

### T-0 — Temporal attack

The Veyr travel backward and attempt to erase humanity's causal development.

Adrian and Mira enter the Stillhouse.

### Post-erasure

Outside AION, humanity no longer exists.

Earth is a Veyr colony.

Adrian and Mira remain causally isolated.

---

## Timeline B — The restored human history

### The temporal deployment

Mira learns how to use the Veyr temporal system.

She travels to a period **before the Veyr invasion and before humanity knows aliens exist**.

Her physical location remains in the sky above Earth.

She releases the virus into the atmosphere / Veyr operational environment.

### The Veyr transmission cascade

The Veyr's warp / FTL transportation network carries infected biological material and personnel between worlds.

Because the Veyr have not yet attacked Earth, Earth does not recognize the outbreak as an alien event.

The infection spreads through Veyr civilization.

### Veyr collapse

The Veyr civilization dies or becomes nonfunctional.

Their temporal operation can no longer sustain the altered history.

The human causal history reasserts itself.

### Adrian emerges

Adrian sees human civilization restored.

He initially believes the Stillhouse worked.

He steps outside.

He discovers:

- the war never happened,
- the Veyr invasion is absent from human history,
- Adrian exists,
- Mira exists,
- they have never met,
- their shared memories have no historical counterpart.

Adrian returns to the Stillhouse.

### The search

Adrian uses the laboratory's surviving information to locate Mira.

The search is difficult because the restored timeline contains no record of their wartime relationship.

Eventually they meet.

Mira recognizes him.

The emotional climax is not their first meeting.

It is the moment they realize that **both remember**.

### The lost twenty years

Their subjective lives contain nearly twenty years of war, research, fear, love and survival.

Their restored world contains none of it.

To everyone else, Adrian and Mira are simply two scientists who have just met.

To each other, they are survivors of a twenty-year history that has been erased.

### The final journey

They decide not to recreate the war.

They use the temporal technology one final time.

They travel to a non-documented era of human history, far enough from recorded civilization that their arrival cannot create a known historical contradiction.

They live together.

They grow old.

They die together.

---

## Causal interpretation

The story is not a simple reset.

The Veyr's temporal intervention creates a self-defeating causal loop:

1. The Veyr fear humanity.
2. They erase humanity's future.
3. Their temporal intervention creates the conditions for AION.
4. AION preserves Adrian and Mira.
5. Mira infects the Veyr before their invasion.
6. The Veyr civilization collapses.
7. The altered timeline loses the cause that sustained the human erasure.
8. Human history reasserts itself.
9. Adrian and Mira survive as ordinary people with extraordinary memories.
10. Their final journey removes them from documented history.

The loop closes without requiring the protagonists to become famous historical figures.
