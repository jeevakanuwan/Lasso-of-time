# Master Timeline

- **T-12 years:** unexplained astronomical events.
- **T-10:** first Veyr arrival.
- **T-9 to T-1:** war; Adrian and Mira join the international science program.
- **T-1:** Mira's team identifies the Veyr vulnerability and develops the virus.
- **T-8 months:** intelligence reveals Veyr temporal research.
- **T-3 months:** Adrian builds AION.
- **T-0:** Veyr activate the temporal attack; original human history is overwritten.
- **After erasure:** Adrian and Mira survive inside AION while Earth becomes a Veyr colony.
- **Final mission:** they carry the virus outside.
- **Boundary event:** the interlock sends Mira to first contact.
- **Capture:** Veyr transport her to their modern era.
- **Outbreak:** the virus spreads across the Veyr civilization.
- **End:** Veyr temporal empire collapses; humanity's ultimate fate remains a deliberate question.
