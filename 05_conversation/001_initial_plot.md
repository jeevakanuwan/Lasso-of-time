# Conversation 001 — Initial Plot

The user proposed a young scientist falling in love with his fellow scientist during an alien invasion. They are developing a virus against the alien race while the aliens seek their own scientific solution.

The aliens eventually use time travel to erase humanity. The male scientist discovers the temporal threat and constructs a Faraday-cage-inspired device that is immune to the temporal anomaly. He and his girlfriend continue working from inside.

Outside the chamber, time has changed: humanity no longer exists and Earth has become an alien colony.

The two remaining humans leave to deliver the virus. When the woman exits, the safety protocols send her into the past, to the moment the aliens first came to Earth. She is captured and taken to the aliens' modern era. The aliens unknowingly release the virus into their civilization, wiping themselves out across the universe.

## Adaptation decisions
- Working title: **The Lasso of Time**.
- Device: **AION — Acausal Isolation and Observer Nullification**, nickname **Stillhouse**.
- It does not literally stop time.
- It maintains a locally self-consistent causal frame.
- Mira's displacement is caused by the Causal Return Interlock.
- The Veyr's attempt to erase humanity becomes the mechanism that carries the virus into their own civilization.
