# Science Framework

## Established science used as a foundation

- Relativity and proper time.
- Electromagnetic shielding.
- Superconductivity.
- Atomic clocks and precision frequency measurement.
- Biological specificity and host-pathogen interactions.
- Epidemiological spread through transportation networks.

## Speculative extensions

These are fictional technologies, not established science:

1. Chronometric phase as a measurable temporal signature.
2. Controlled closed-timelike-trajectory effects.
3. Causal boundary engineering.
4. AION's locally self-consistent causal frame.
5. Temporal movement of matter into an earlier history.
6. A timeline reassertion triggered by the collapse of the Veyr causal branch.
7. A Veyr-specific viral agent capable of spreading through their FTL logistics network.

## The FTL transmission rule

Do not suggest that a warp field automatically infects distant planets. The virus spreads because infected Veyr personnel and biological cargo physically travel aboard ships. FTL makes those journeys fast and frequent, allowing infected carriers to reach distant systems before the outbreak is recognized.

The virus's fictional biology must explain why it can remain undetected during transit and why the Veyr's normal screening fails.

## Writing rule

Characters should distinguish measurement from interpretation. Use phrases such as:
- “The model predicts…”
- “If the Veyr are right…”
- “We measured the effect; we have not explained it.”
- “That part is theory.”

The story should feel scientifically disciplined even when it uses speculative physics.
