# Science Framework

## Established physics used directly
- Relativity and proper time.
- Electromagnetic shielding.
- Superconductivity.
- Atomic clocks.
- Quantum measurement concepts.
- Biological specificity and host-pathogen interactions.

## Speculative extensions
- Chronometric phase.
- Controlled closed-timelike-trajectory effects.
- Causal boundary engineering.
- AION's local self-consistent causal frame.
- Biological transmission across altered causal histories.

Characters should distinguish what they know from what they infer. Established physics should not be presented as proof of the speculative mechanisms.
