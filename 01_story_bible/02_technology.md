# Technology Bible

## AION — Acausal Isolation and Observer Nullification

### Nickname: The Stillhouse

AION is not a conventional Faraday cage and does not literally block time. It borrows the Faraday cage's principle of a controlled boundary but applies it to a fictional causal reference field.

### Components

1. A superconducting toroidal lattice.
2. A metamaterial shell controlling electromagnetic and gravitational coupling at small scales.
3. Distributed atomic clocks and optical frequency references.
4. A high-energy stabilization ring.
5. A redundant computer system solving for a self-consistent local proper-time frame.
6. A temporal phase-lock system that resists externally imposed changes to the chamber's causal boundary conditions.

### Speculative mechanism

The Veyr have learned to manipulate quantum fields around closed timelike trajectories, producing a measurable fictional **chronometric phase**: a relationship between local proper time and the surrounding spacetime history.

AION does not travel through time. It maintains a locally self-consistent causal solution while the external solution changes.

The analogy: a tiny island whose coordinates remain defined while the map beneath it is redrawn.

### Why the Veyr cannot detect it

The Veyr's temporal sensors identify matter partly through chronometric signatures. AION nulls that signature at its boundary. It is not optically invisible; it is difficult to detect with the Veyr's temporal sensing method.

### What time is like inside

Time does not stop inside. Clocks tick, people age and experiments run normally. What changes is the history to which the chamber is causally connected.

### Causal Return Interlock

Crossing the boundary during a major temporal rewrite is dangerous. AION's Causal Return Interlock attempts to resolve an object whose causal signature is incompatible with the active timeline.

The interlock can interface with the Veyr temporal system, but its destination is not a simple preset. Mira eventually learns to use the Veyr device intentionally, allowing her to choose a pre-invasion period and deploy the virus.

### Limits

- Enormous energy demand.
- Severe heat-management challenges.
- The chamber cannot be moved easily.
- It cannot restore history by itself.
- Boundary crossing can separate people across timelines.
- It preserves a local causal reference state, not the entire previous universe.

### Governing rule

**AION preserves observers, not history.**
