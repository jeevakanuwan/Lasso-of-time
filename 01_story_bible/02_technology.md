# Technology Bible — AION / The Stillhouse

## AION
**Acausal Isolation and Observer Nullification.** Nickname: **the Stillhouse**.

AION is not a conventional Faraday cage and does not literally block time. A Faraday cage inspires its architecture: a closed boundary whose controlled physical state dominates conditions inside it.

AION uses a superconducting toroidal lattice, metamaterial shell, atomic clocks, optical frequency references, a high-energy stabilization ring and a control computer maintaining a locally self-consistent proper-time frame.

The aliens' temporal technology couples to a fictional **chronometric phase**. AION nulls that phase at its boundary, making the chamber appear causally blank to Veyr sensors.

**Rule: AION preserves observers, not history.**

## Causal Return Interlock
Crossing the boundary while the outside timeline is incompatible with a person's causal reference state is dangerous. Instead of allowing a paradox, the system seeks the nearest stable historical state compatible with the traveler. For Mira, that anchor is first contact.

## Limitations
Huge energy demand, difficult heat rejection, immobility, limited capacity, dangerous boundary crossing, and no ability to restore the original timeline automatically.
