# Characters

## Dr. Adrian Sen

Young theoretical physicist and temporal systems scientist.

- Brilliant, methodical and emotionally guarded.
- Treats causality as a structure that can be measured.
- Initially believes every problem can be solved if enough variables are controlled.
- Tries to protect Mira by controlling outcomes.
- Discovers the Veyr temporal attack and builds AION.
- Survives the rewritten timeline inside the Stillhouse.
- After history returns, he discovers that his ordinary self exists but has never met Mira.
- Chooses to search for her rather than accept the restoration as a complete victory.

### Arc

Adrian begins by trying to save civilization. He ends by accepting that history may not remember his sacrifice, and that the most important thing he can recover is the person who shared it.

## Dr. Mira Vale

Molecular biologist and xenobiologist; Adrian's fellow scientist and love.

- Experimentally fearless and attentive to biological detail.
- Leads development of the virus against the Veyr.
- Challenges Adrian when his equations obscure human consequences.
- Understands that the virus must exploit a biological dependency specific to the Veyr.
- Learns to operate the Veyr temporal technology.
- Travels to a pre-invasion era and deploys the virus from above Earth.
- Understands that the Veyr's own FTL network can distribute the pathogen across their civilization.

### Arc

Mira begins believing the virus is a weapon. She comes to see it as the only remaining means of preventing the war from ever happening.

## The Veyr

A technologically superior interstellar civilization with advanced biological engineering, temporal technology and FTL transportation.

They are not irrational monsters. Their temporal attack is the logical extreme of strategic desperation: they believe a war can be won by preventing the enemy from ever becoming an enemy.

Their greatest blind spot is that they treat temporal history as editable and their own infrastructure as invulnerable.

## Supporting characters

Supporting characters should show the human cost of the original war and the emotional isolation of a restored world that has no record of it. Avoid allowing secondary characters to distract from Adrian and Mira's central relationship.
