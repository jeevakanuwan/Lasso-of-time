# Characters

## Dr. Adrian Sen
Young theoretical physicist and temporal-systems scientist. Brilliant, methodical and convinced that enough mathematics can control uncertainty. His weakness is trying to protect Mira by controlling outcomes.

**Arc:** from trying to save civilization to accepting that he may only be able to save the possibility of civilization.

## Dr. Mira Vale
Molecular biologist and xenobiologist. Intuitive, experimentally fearless and more comfortable with biological uncertainty than Adrian is. She leads development of the species-specific viral agent.

**Arc:** from seeing the virus as a weapon to understanding it as humanity's final message to the species that tried to erase it.

## The Veyr
A technologically superior extraterrestrial civilization. They are not irrational monsters; their temporal attack is the logical extreme of strategic desperation.

Their doctrine: **prevent the enemy from ever becoming an enemy.**

## Veyr scientists
Give them individual motives. At least one should genuinely believe the temporal operation is containment rather than genocide.
