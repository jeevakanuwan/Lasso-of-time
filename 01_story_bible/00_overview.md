# Story Overview

## Working title

**The Lasso of Time**

## Genre

Hard-ish science fiction / military science fiction / temporal thriller / tragic romance.

## Core premise

Humanity is fighting an extraterrestrial civilization called the Veyr. Two young scientists, **Dr. Adrian Sen** and **Dr. Mira Vale**, fall in love while developing a biological weapon against the invaders.

The Veyr discover temporal manipulation and decide that the safest way to win is not to conquer humanity, but to travel backward and prevent humanity from ever becoming a civilization capable of threatening them.

Adrian discovers the temporal attack and builds the **AION — Acausal Isolation and Observer Nullification**, known as the **Stillhouse**. It creates a locally self-consistent causal frame that remains coherent while the external timeline is rewritten.

The Veyr succeed.

Outside the Stillhouse, humanity disappears from history and Earth becomes a Veyr colony.

Adrian and Mira survive inside for what feels like years. They eventually discover that Mira can exploit the Veyr temporal technology. She travels backward to a period long before the invasion and deploys the virus from the sky above Earth.

The virus does not need Earth to know the Veyr exist.

The Veyr's own faster-than-light / warp transportation network becomes the transmission system. Every ship, colony and biological supply route spreads the pathogen through the Veyr civilization.

The Veyr collapse.

The original human history reasserts itself.

Adrian steps out of the Stillhouse and discovers a world in which the war never happened. He and Mira already exist in this restored history, but they have never met.

He returns to the Stillhouse rather than accepting a world in which their shared life has been erased.

After an extended search, they find one another.

They remember everything.

History does not.

The two spend the equivalent of almost twenty years searching, fighting and surviving a war that the restored universe says never happened.

Eventually they choose not to interfere with the restored world.

Instead, they use the recovered temporal technology to travel to a **non-documented period of human prehistory**, far outside the historical record.

There, free from the machinery of the war and from the causal pressures that shaped their lives, they live together until the end.

## Central dramatic question

If history forgets your greatest sacrifice, does that sacrifice become meaningless?

## Final answer

No.

The universe remembers through consequences, even when people do not.

Adrian and Mira's relationship survives not because history preserves it, but because they do.

## Ending principle

The ending should be bittersweet, intimate and strangely peaceful.

Humanity gets its ordinary future.

The protagonists get each other.

Their war becomes a secret carried by two people who know it happened.
