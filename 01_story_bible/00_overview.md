# Story Overview

## Working title
**The Lasso of Time**

## Genre
Hard-ish science fiction / military science fiction / temporal thriller / tragic romance.

## Core premise
Humanity is losing a war against an extraterrestrial civilization. Two scientists, **Dr. Adrian Sen** and **Dr. Mira Vale**, develop a biological weapon. Before humanity can deploy it, the aliens discover temporal manipulation and attempt to erase humanity from its origin.

Adrian discovers the attack and builds the **AION Stillhouse**, preserving its occupants from changes to external history. When the outside universe is rewritten, Adrian and Mira survive inside.

They eventually leave to deliver the virus. The Stillhouse safety system sends Mira to the first alien arrival on Earth. The aliens capture her and transport her into their rewritten present, unknowingly carrying the virus into the heart of their civilization.
