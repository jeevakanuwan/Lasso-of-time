# The Veyr

The Veyr are a distributed biological civilization with a conserved molecular pathway absent in humans. Mira's virus hijacks this pathway.

They possess superior materials science, temporal engineering, interstellar travel and biological engineering. Humanity's advantages are improvisation and prolonged observation of Veyr biology.

When conventional warfare becomes too costly, the Veyr decide to remove humanity from the causal chain entirely. Their fatal mistake is assuming temporal causality can be edited cleanly and failing to understand AION or the biological payload carried through it.
