# Themes

- **Love against causality:** two people remain emotionally human while history becomes negotiable.
- **Scientific responsibility:** discoveries are neither good nor evil; consequences escape laboratories.
- **The arrogance of erasure:** deleting an enemy's beginning may create the conditions for its final victory.
- **Memory:** remembering a vanished civilization becomes evidence that it existed.
- **The paradox of victory:** humanity's victory may look like extinction from inside the original timeline.
