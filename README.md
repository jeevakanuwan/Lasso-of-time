# The Lasso of Time

A science-fiction novel about love, extinction, temporal warfare, and two people who save a world that will never know they did.

## Premise

Humanity is losing a war against the Veyr, an extraterrestrial civilization. Scientists **Dr. Adrian Sen** and **Dr. Mira Vale** fall in love while developing a virus that can exploit a Veyr biological vulnerability.

The Veyr attempt to erase humanity by changing the past. Adrian builds the **AION — Acausal Isolation and Observer Nullification** chamber, nicknamed the **Stillhouse**, which preserves its occupants' causal reference state while the outside timeline is rewritten.

When humanity disappears from history, Adrian and Mira survive inside the Stillhouse. Mira eventually uses Veyr temporal technology to travel to a time before the invasion and deploy the virus from above Earth. The Veyr's own FTL transport network spreads the infection through their civilization.

Human history returns. Adrian and Mira still exist, but in the restored timeline they have never met. They must find each other again, carrying memories of almost twenty years of a war no one else remembers. In the end, they leave documented history and live together in an undocumented era of human prehistory.

- `07_revisions/` — continuity checks and revision history.

## Canon rules

- AION preserves observers, not history.
- The Veyr's FTL network is the virus's interstellar transmission pathway.
- Earth has no knowledge of the Veyr when Mira deploys the virus.
- Adrian and Mira retain their memories after human history returns.
- The restored versions of Adrian and Mira have never met before their reunion.
- Their final departure into undocumented prehistory is voluntary.

The story should feel scientifically disciplined even when it uses speculative physics. Established science and fictional extrapolation must be distinguished.
